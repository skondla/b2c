# Mortgage Loan Products Guide

## Conventional Loans

### 30-Year Fixed Conventional
The most popular mortgage in the United States. Your interest rate stays the same for all 30 years, so your principal and interest payment never changes.

**Eligibility Requirements:**
- Minimum FICO credit score: 620
- Maximum debt-to-income ratio (DTI): 45%
- Maximum loan-to-value (LTV): 97% (3% minimum down payment)
- Maximum loan amount: $766,550 (2024 conforming limit for most counties)
- Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) required if LTV > 80%

**Who It's Best For:**
- First-time homebuyers with limited savings
- Buyers who want the lowest possible monthly payment
- Borrowers planning to stay in the home 7+ years

**Key Benefits:**
- Predictable monthly payments (rate never changes)
- PMI automatically cancels at 80% LTV (Homeowners Protection Act)
- No prepayment penalty
- Can be refinanced at any time

**Costs:**
- PMI typically 0.5%–1.5% of loan amount per year if LTV > 80%
- Total interest paid is higher than shorter-term loans

---

### 15-Year Fixed Conventional
Pay off your home in half the time. Interest rates are typically 0.5%–0.75% lower than the 30-year, saving substantial interest over the life of the loan.

**Eligibility Requirements:**
- Minimum FICO: 620
- Maximum DTI: 43%
- Maximum LTV: 97%
- Maximum loan amount: $766,550

**Who It's Best For:**
- Borrowers who can afford higher monthly payments
- Those wanting to build equity faster
- Refinancers looking to shorten remaining loan term

---

### 5/1 Adjustable-Rate Mortgage (ARM)
Fixed rate for the first 5 years, then adjusts annually based on market index (SOFR).

**Eligibility:**
- Minimum FICO: 640
- Maximum DTI: 43%

**Rate Caps:** 2% initial adjustment cap, 2% periodic cap, 5% lifetime cap.

## Government-Backed Loans

### FHA Loan (30-Year Fixed)
Insured by the Federal Housing Administration. Allows lower credit scores and smaller down payments than conventional loans.

**Eligibility Requirements:**
- Minimum FICO 580: 3.5% down payment required
- FICO 500–579: 10% down payment required
- FICO below 500: not eligible for FHA
- Maximum DTI: 57% (with compensating factors)
- Maximum LTV: 96.5% (3.5% down)
- Maximum loan amount: varies by county (most areas: $498,257; high-cost: $1,149,825)

**Mortgage Insurance Premiums (MIP):**
- Upfront MIP: 1.75% of loan amount (can be financed into loan)
- Annual MIP: 0.55% per year for 30-year loans with < 10% down (paid monthly, for life of loan)
- Annual MIP: 0.55% for 11 years if down payment ≥ 10%

**Who It's Best For:**
- First-time buyers with credit scores 580–659
- Borrowers with limited savings for down payment
- Those with higher debt loads

**Important Note:** FHA MIP cannot be removed if down payment was less than 10%. Borrowers may need to refinance to a conventional loan to eliminate MIP once equity reaches 20%.

---

### VA Loan (30-Year Fixed)
Guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Available to eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and qualifying surviving spouses.

**Eligibility Requirements:**
- Must have VA loan entitlement (Certificate of Eligibility from VA)
- Minimum FICO recommended: 580–620 (lender overlay)
- Maximum DTI: 60% (with residual income analysis)
- No maximum loan amount (subject to lender limits)
- No down payment required
- No private mortgage insurance

**VA Funding Fee:**
- First use, no down payment: 2.3% of loan amount
- First use, 5%+ down: 1.65%
- First use, 10%+ down: 1.4%
- Subsequent use: 3.6% (no down payment)
- Exempt: veterans with service-connected disability rating

**VA Minimum Property Requirements (MPRs):**
Properties must be safe, structurally sound, and sanitary. VA appraiser will flag any MPR issues.

**Who It's Best For:**
- Any eligible veteran or active-duty service member — typically the best mortgage available to qualified borrowers

---

### Jumbo Loan (30-Year Fixed)
For loan amounts above the conforming limit ($766,550 in most areas). Not backed by Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac.

**Eligibility Requirements:**
- Minimum FICO: 700–720
- Maximum DTI: 43%
- Maximum LTV: 80% (20% minimum down payment)
- Minimum loan amount: $766,551
- Maximum loan amount: $2,000,000–$5,000,000 (lender-specific)
- Reserves: typically 12 months PITI in liquid assets

**Who It's Best For:**
- High-income borrowers in high-cost real estate markets (California, New York, etc.)
- Buyers of luxury properties

**Key Difference from Conforming:** Stricter qualification, higher down payment, but often competitive interest rates for well-qualified borrowers.

## Loan Type Comparison

| Feature | Conventional | FHA | VA | Jumbo |
|---------|-------------|-----|-----|-------|
| Min FICO | 620 | 500 | 580 | 700 |
| Min Down | 3% | 3.5% | 0% | 20% |
| Max DTI | 45% | 57% | 60% | 43% |
| Mortgage Insurance | PMI (removable) | MIP (may be permanent) | None | None |
| Loan Limit | $766,550 | County-specific | None | >$766,550 |

## Choosing the Right Product

**If you're a veteran:** Always start with VA — no down payment, no PMI.

**If your FICO is 580–659:** FHA is likely your best option. Consider rebuilding credit and refinancing to conventional later.

**If your FICO is 660+:** Compare conventional vs. FHA. At higher scores, conventional is often cheaper (no lifetime MIP).

**If your loan amount exceeds $766,550:** Jumbo is your only option — need 20% down and strong qualifications.

**If you want the lowest payment today:** 30-year fixed.

**If you want to pay off faster and save interest:** 15-year fixed or ARM (if you'll sell before adjustment).
